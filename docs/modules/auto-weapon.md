# Auto Weapon

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Project README](../../README.md)

Module: **auto-weapon** (Meteor upgrade; the original and Comet entries share state).

Comet extends Meteor Auto Weapon with main-inventory selection. Enable **use-inventory** to consider weapons outside the hotbar; **inventory-min-gain** defaults to 1 damage and avoids unnecessary swaps.

See [inventory loans](../guides/inventory-loans.md) for transfer/restoration rules, shared-loan coordination and server limitations.
