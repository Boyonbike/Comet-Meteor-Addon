# Auto Lobby

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Project README](../../README.md)

Module: **auto-lobby**.

Auto Lobby restores a saved Highway Fly trip after a disconnect, whether the server dropped you or you pressed **Disconnect** yourself. Enable it independently, or enable Highway Fly’s **Integrations → lobby-recovery** to request it during a trip. It writes a small marker when the world unloads and reads it again on the next login, so a normal login also works. With **auto-reconnect** on (default), an unexpected connection loss retries the same server after **reconnect-delay** seconds (default **3.5**, minimum 1), then lobby recovery resumes the trip. Consecutive failures use exponential backoff with jitter, capped at 60 seconds or a higher configured base delay. Five minutes of a stable connection reset that backoff. It uses Meteor's reconnect screen and restores the prior reconnect preference and delay when finished. A deliberate Highway Fly durability logout never retries. The manual **Disconnect** button stays in the menu until you choose to log in again. Cancelling the retry screen or choosing another server releases Highway Fly's automatic reconnect. The marker is tied to the server address (singleplayer is its own entry), so a trip is not applied to a different server. It only arms while flight or recovery is running. Turning Highway Fly off, arriving, or its low-durability safety logout ends the trip and cancels recovery. During lobby recovery, flight steering, mouse capture, home commands and portal blocking are suspended; they resume only when flight restarts.

- After the next join, it waits **join-delay** (default 40 ticks) before scanning a lobby for a portal. If you are already in the flight's dimension, it restarts flight after about a second. A normal login after a manual disconnect resumes the same way; only the marker file is needed.
- Known recovery spawns are recognised by dimension and position: **The End `1000.5 100 1000.5`** is the cracked lobby (log in, then walk to a portal), **Overworld `247.5 62 228.5`** is the main lobby (walk to a portal), and **The End `29.5 19.06 18.5`** is the waiting area (stay still until the server transfers you). Detection allows two blocks horizontally and one vertically; the recognised area is retained while walking to its portal and reset on world changes. These areas never count as returning to the route, even when the saved route uses the same dimension. Dimension changes during flight hand control to recovery when enabled.
- The known cracked lobby automatically uses **login-password**, regardless of **cracked-account**. A blank password pauses movement until you set a password or log in manually. The main lobby and waiting area do not send `/login`. **cracked-account** (default off) additionally handles lobby servers that need `/login` for a cracked account. It sends `/login` plus **login-password** after **join-delay**, then waits up to **login-timeout** (default 200 ticks, 10 seconds) for a server confirmation before starting the usual portal search. A rejected password warns once and waits for you to log in manually; no confirmation only warns and continues. Confirmations are recognised only in server chat, not player chat, and the password is kept out of recent-chat history. The password is stored in plain text in the Meteor config.
- Turning **lobby-recovery** off while a trip is paused ends Highway Fly and cancels its pending recovery. Turning **auto-reconnect** off stops its automatic retry while leaving a manual login available.
- The trip server, dimension, destination and altitude are saved atomically to `meteor-client/auto-lobby-recovery.json` as flight starts and refreshed on an interrupted disconnect. This preserves trip identity through a client crash or restart. An unreadable marker pauses startup instead of starting a route on an unknown server; profile loading preserves the marker. If you log into a different server first, the marker is left alone for the server it belongs to. Turning Highway Fly off clears its pending marker; an independently enabled Auto Lobby stays enabled and waits for another saved trip. As before, a marker for another server is kept while that server is not open, and deleting the file cancels it.
- If the join is a lobby or an unrecognised recovery area in another dimension, it searches loaded chunks for the nearest **Nether portal** block within **search-radius** (default 64) and **search-height** (default 32). It never loads a chunk; increase the radius if the portal is loaded but outside the scan.
- **use-baritone** (default on) has Baritone's path manager walk to the portal, then the last few blocks are walked directly. Without Baritone it turns toward the portal and walks, jumping over a frame or a step. If it cannot move for five seconds it warns and tries other portal candidates, temporarily avoiding the failed portal. If no usable loaded portal exists, it keeps waiting and rescanning indefinitely for the restart area to clear.
- Once in the portal it stops moving and waits for the dimension change. If the destination places the player inside the exit portal, recovery finds a loaded, collision-free exit with floor support throughout the walk and rechecks that support every tick before starting flight. A portal can take a few seconds; it warns once after 10 seconds.
- If no portal is loaded, it warns once and rescans every **rescan-delay** (default 100 ticks). It resumes as soon as a join or server transfer puts you back in the flight's dimension; you do not need to restart the module by hand.
- **resume-flight** (default on) restarts flight; turning it off ends Highway Fly after returning to the route dimension. **enter-portal** (default on) controls whether it tries the portal at all. Turn **enter-portal** off to only wait for a direct rejoin.
- The **auto-set-home** helper resumes with flight if its switch remains on; a disabled helper stays disabled. Homes are never updated in the lobby. The **auto-set-home-timer** HUD shows its current mode and countdown while it can act.
- It pauses flight on entering the waiting area and keeps the configured **destination**. Flight restart still checks equipment and conflicting controllers and can equip a spare elytra through **swap-elytra**.
- The module list shows `Waiting for join`, `Settling`, `Logging in`, `Waiting area`, `Finding portal`, `Walking to portal`, `Entering portal`, `Waiting for Nether`, `Resuming flight` or `Done`.
- **No in-game verification yet.** Portal selection and the disconnect marker are unit-tested (nearest loaded block, bounds, negative coordinates, unloaded chunks, palette gating, tie-breaks, server scoping, corrupt markers and atomic replacement); the login reply patterns and known spawn detection are unit-tested too. Walking into a live waiting-area portal, manual-disconnect recovery, the rejoin resume and a live cracked-account login (including a rejected password) still need gameplay testing.

**Standalone Auto Lobby:** enable it to recover an existing saved trip while Highway Fly is off. Without a saved trip it shows **Waiting for saved trip** and does not choose a destination or start flight. Login, portal search and the existing **resume-flight** behavior are preserved; broader standalone recovery will be redesigned separately.

**Standalone Anti Portal:** enable it to protect ordinary movement without Highway Fly. Its Nether/End portal and notification settings live in its own module. Protection yields during lobby recovery so the recovery portal remains usable. Flight portal avoidance and crossing settings stay in Highway Fly.

## Independent and trip-managed activation

Enable this helper independently in Comet or allow Highway Fly to request it through Integrations. Stopping Highway Fly releases its request and preserves independent activation. **Keep enabled independently** retains a trip-managed helper after the trip; explicitly disabling one also disables its Highway Fly integration. See [Highway Fly](highway-fly.md).
